The Last-Mile Dissemination Crisis

India has world-class meteorological models, but data is inaccessible to ground stakeholders.
The Challenge

⚠️ Meteorological Jargon

Official IMD bulletins use synoptic terminology ("convective instability", "isobaric trough", "TAF/METAR") that 95% of rural farmers and fishermen cannot comprehend.

  • Delayed protective action
  • Costly fertilizer wash-off
  • Maritime safety hazards

🌐 Vernacular & Literacy Gap

Most modern weather apps are in English and text-heavy. India's coastal fishermen and agricultural workforce require natural voice in their regional dialects.

  • No two-way conversational voice
  • Lack of regional dialect support
  • Zero interactive audio playback

⚡ Disaster Zone Blackouts

When severe tropical cyclones or heavy floods strike, local cell towers collapse. Cloud-reliant weather apps freeze, leaving frontline victims stranded without alerts.

  • Critical communication breakdown
  • No offline local caching
  • Loss of emergency advisories

Introducing WeatherGPT

An end-to-end multi-sector conversational AI & real-time GIS platform.
Core Solution

💬 Sector-Aware Conversational AI

WeatherGPT synthesizes raw atmospheric data into tailored, actionable guidance matching 6 distinct user personas (Farmers, Pilots, Fishermen, Urban Planners, Researchers, and Citizens).

  • Interactive 5-day inline forecast cards
  • Sector-specific protective advisories
  • One-click prompt suggestions tailored by persona

🗣️ 6 Indian Languages + Voice Engine

Full voice input (`SpeechRecognizer`) with real-time audio waveform visualizer and Text-to-Speech (`TTS`) read-aloud in English, हिन्दी, తెలుగు, தமிழ், বাংলা, and मराठी.

  • Hands-free interaction for fieldwork
  • Dialect-aware conversational responses
  • Voice auto-play setting for accessibility

🗺️ Interactive Doppler Radar & GIS Tracking

Native canvas visualization of India's Doppler Weather Radar (DWR) stations with layer toggles for Radar Reflectivity (dBZ), Cyclone Track & Cone of Uncertainty, and Nowcast playback.

🛡️ Offline-First Resilient Architecture

Built on a local Room Database and network observers. When cellular connectivity drops during a storm, the app automatically switches to cached alerts, radar frames, and safety guides.

Persona Matrix

🌾 Farmer / Krishi

Focus: Aerodrome Met & Airfield Safety

  • METAR/TAF briefings & SIGMET warnings

🚢 Marine & Coastal

Focus: INCOIS Maritime Safety

  • Significant wave heights (3.5m - 4.5m)

🏙️ Urban Planner

🔬 Climate Researcher

Focus: Synoptic Dynamics & Modeling

  • GFS vs NCUM/WRF model ensemble spread

👥 General Public

Focus: Daily Life & Outdoor Routines

  • Hour-by-hour rain probability percentages
  • UV index & heatwave precautions
  • 5-day visual temperature trend cards

GIS Radar & Official IMD Alert Protocol

Visual tracking and standardized 4-tier disaster notification system.
Disaster Management

📡 DWR Doppler Radar & Cyclone Tracking

Interactive GIS visualizer mapping national radar stations (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Visakhapatnam):

  • Radar Reflectivity (dBZ): Convective storm cells
  • Cyclone Landfall Cone: Visual uncertainty track
  • Rainfall Accumulation: Multi-day mm metrics
  • Surface Wind Vectors: Wind streamline flow
  • Playback Time Slider: -3h past to +1h WRF model nowcast

🚨 Official IMD Color-Coded Alert Protocol

Standardized disaster warnings integrated with real-time WebSockets and high-priority heads-up device notifications:

🔴 RED (WARNING): Take Action — Flash flood (>200mm) or Severe Cyclone.
🟠 ORANGE (ALERT): Be Prepared — Heatwaves & squally gales (65 km/h).
🟡 YELLOW (WATCH): Be Updated — Thunderstorms and lightning strikes.
🟢 GREEN (NORMAL): No Warning — Safe seasonal conditions.

System Architecture & Engineering Rigor

Modern Android 14+ Jetpack Compose client + Scalable FastAPI async backend.
Tech Stack

📱 Android Native UI Layer

  • Jetpack Compose: 100% declarative UI
  • Adaptive Navigation Suite: Phones & tablets
  • Dagger Hilt: Clean Dependency Injection
  • Coroutines & Flow: Reactive state management

⚡ Async Backend & WebSockets

  • FastAPI (Python): Non-blocking async endpoints
  • WebSocket Channels: Instant nowcast pushes
  • Pydantic v2: Strict data contracts
  • RAG Grounding: Prevents LLM hallucinations

🛡️ Offline-First Data Pipeline

  • Room Database: Local SQLite data persistence
  • DataStore: User preferences & roles
  • ConnectivityObserver: Auto offline transition
  • NotificationManager: High-priority alerts
